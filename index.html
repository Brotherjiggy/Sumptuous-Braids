FAQ

Before your appointment.

Do I need to bring my own hair?

Hair requirements vary by service. Please confirm whether hair is included when making your appointment.

How should I prepare my hair?

We recommend arriving with clean, fully detangled and stretched hair unless your appointment includes a preparation service.

How long will my appointment take?

Appointment times depend on the style, size, length and amount of hair. Your estimated time will be provided during booking.

Can I bring an inspiration photo?

Absolutely. Inspiration photos help us understand your preferred size, length, pattern and overall look.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please contact Sumptuous Braids as early as possible if you need to change or cancel your appointment. Your final cancellation and deposit policy can be provided during booking.
